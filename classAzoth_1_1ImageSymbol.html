Detailed Description

Represents a symbol associated with a loaded image.

ImageSymbol describes a named address inside a module. Symbols may come from different sources such as export tables, import tables, or analysis results produced by the framework.

Depending on the source, some fields may not be populated (e.g. forwarder only applies to forwarded exports).
